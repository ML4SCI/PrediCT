# D036.1 Detectability floor amendment: larger separations, GMM arm, interpretable units

**Date**: 2026-10-02
**Stage**: discover (validation of D021)
**Status**: Active. Written after the D036 first run and its diagnosis, before any D036.1 run. D036 and its first-run result stand unchanged.
**Module**: `scripts/probe_detectability_floor.py --amendment`, `submit_detectability.sh` (`AMEND=1`)

## Why

The D036 first run found zero detection in every setting, including two groups six background SDs apart on a gaussian background. The diagnosis (`scripts/diagnose_detectability.py`) showed the instrument is correct: the planted separation survives re-scaling and PCA, and a one-dimensional oracle recovers it. Euclidean kmeans and ward on the unwhitened PCs instead split the dominant first PC (33% of the variance, SD 3.4 times a typical direction), whose two-way cut explains more variance than a 6-SD planted split in a low-variance direction. D036's pre-registered interpretation 3 has been applied to the kmeans / ward detector: Finding 1's "no discrete phenotypes" is withdrawn in its current form.

Two questions remain: where the kmeans / ward floor actually is, and whether the third D021 algorithm (Gaussian mixture, full covariance), which D036 left out for cost, passes the positive control. A full-covariance mixture can model the elongated burden axis as one component and is not bound to Euclidean cuts.

## Changes (everything else as in D036)

- **delta grid**: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16. Deltas up to 6 reuse the D036 seeds; kmeans and ward there must reproduce the first run exactly (reported as a reproducibility count).
- **GMM arm**: gap statistic with the D021 GMM (full covariance, n_init 5) on the same PCs and bootstraps (B = 100), and forced-k GMM labels with ARI against the planted labels and Hennig clusterboot (GMM, 100 bootstraps).
- **Units**: each replicate also records the separation in PC1-SD units (delta x mean background SD along the planting axes / sqrt of the largest background eigenvalue) and the planted between-group share of total variance after re-scaling.
- Output in `<root>/detectability_d0361/`; the D036 output is not touched.

## Rules (fixed before running)

- Floors exactly as D036 (smallest delta > 0 with rate >= 0.80), for: kmeans gap (primary, unchanged), ward gap, kmeans recovered, GMM gap (`detected_gmm`), GMM recovered (`recovered_gmm`: forced-k GMM ARI >= 0.80 and every cluster Hennig median Jaccard >= 0.75).
- **Detector that passes the positive control**: an arm whose gaussian-background floor is finite in the random-direction, balanced setting for every k_true.

**Clarification (2026-10-02, after the 2-replicate smoke run, before the full run).** "Arm" in the pass rule means a k-discovery arm: the kmeans, ward or GMM gap statistic. The forced-k recovery floors (`recovered`, `recovered_gmm`) are told k_true and cannot discover k; they are reported as separability floors (how far apart groups must be before a k-means / GMM partition at the right k recovers them), not as passing the positive control. This is the stricter reading. The smoke run showed forced-k recovery at delta 16 with no gap arm selecting k_true; the summary now also prints the gap-selected k per delta, a reporting addition that changes no rule.

## Interpretation (fixed before running)

1. **GMM passes the positive control and its real-background floor is finite**: Finding 1 is restated with the GMM detector: "2 to 4 groups separated by at least delta* background SD (X PC1 SDs) would have been detected by the GMM gap statistic with at least 80% probability; on the real data the GMM gap selects k = (observed), so no such groups exist." The kmeans / ward blindness off the dominant axis is reported as a methods finding.
2. **GMM passes on gaussian but its real floor is "none"**: no D021 detector resolves planted groups on top of the real background; Finding 1 stays withdrawn as a discreteness claim and is reported as "no structure resolvable by the D021 detectors", together with the gaussian floors.
3. **No arm passes on gaussian at any delta up to 16**: the D021 discovery stack cannot detect separated groups in this geometry at all; Finding 1 is reported only as the observed gap behaviour, and the detector failure is the result.
4. The kmeans / ward floors at 8 to 16 are reported in both delta and PC1-SD units whatever the outcome; the separation at which the planted split overtakes the PC1 cut (planted variance share vs about 0.21) is the expected crossover and is reported, not tuned.

## Verified by

- Reproducibility count (deltas <= 6 vs D036 first run, same seeds).
- Smoke run: `--amendment --smoke` (k 3, deltas 0 / 4 / 16, 2 reps, B 20).
