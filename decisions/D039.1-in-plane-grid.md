# D039.1 Amendment: the selected series must have one in-plane grid

**Date**: 2026-10-03
**Stage**: io
**Status**: Active (amends D039; D039 itself is unchanged)
**Module**: `src/predict/io/dicom_loader.py` (`check_geometry`)

## Decision

`check_geometry` additionally requires every file of the selected series to share one in-plane grid:

1. `PixelSpacing` present and within 1e-4 mm of the first slice's;
2. the same `Rows` x `Columns`;
3. `ImagePositionPatient` (x, y) within 0.05 mm of the first slice's.

A failure is an `irregular_geometry` exclusion at discovery like any other D039 failure, and both loaders raise. The loader's existing check that SimpleITK's z spacing equals the checked z step stays as a second line of defence.

## Rationale

Under D039 alone, pid 156 was included at discovery (regular 3 mm z steps) and then rejected it in stage 2: the loader's SimpleITK spacing check fired (3.79344 mm assigned versus 3.0 mm checked). The cause: 156's selected series mixes two pixel spacings (0.38086 and 0.59570 mm) with an in-plane origin shift. SimpleITK measures the slice spacing in 3D, so an (x, y) jump corrupts the z spacing even when the z steps are regular, and one volume cannot have two pixel spacings in any case.

D039 checked only the z axis, so discovery and the loader disagreed on eligibility. The fix is in discovery (the same check, now complete), not a relaxed loader. The values are not tuning: a volume has exactly one in-plane grid; the tolerances only absorb float formatting in the headers. `scripts/diagnose_slice_geometry.py` reports, for every manifest patient, the distinct pixel spacings and matrices and the largest (x, y) origin deviation, to confirm before the rerun that the check removes 156 and that the origin tolerance does not touch any other series.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep only the slices on the majority grid.** Rejected for the reason D039 rejected merging: repairing a series by hand-picking its files trusts a grouping the data has already shown to be unreliable.
- **Relax the loader's SimpleITK check.** Rejected: it caught a real defect that D039 missed.

## Consequence

Cohort 440 to 439 (156 excluded).
